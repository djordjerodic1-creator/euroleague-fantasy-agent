# Team update inbox

Write your changes below the line in plain words, then commit (you can do this from the GitHub app
or github.com on your phone). The next analyst run applies them to my_team.json, clears this file,
and builds the report on the new team. Only the bank really needs to be exact: prices are read
from the site.

Examples:
- `bank 1.3`
- `sold Nunn, Diarra, Madar; bought Carlik Jones, Jekiri, Justin Robinson; coach Zalgiris; bank 0.2`
- `captain Vezenkov, 6th man Feliz, bench Faye Davidovac Brizuela Joksimovic`
- `did Variation A exactly, bank 0.4`

Don't put your username, email or anything private here: the repo is public.
