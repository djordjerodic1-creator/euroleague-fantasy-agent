Absences from box scores update automatically every day. Injury news is checked before every round (Tuesday and Thursday) from club statements, the official EuroLeague injury report and reliable sports media. What does each status mean? Injured: out with an injury. Out: not playing for another reason (e.g. a club decision). Doubtful: may play, decided close to tip-off. Missed recent games: has not played in his team’s last 2+ games, without a confirmed report. When does a player leave the list? As soon as he plays a EuroLeague game again, or when sources report he is available again. Do injuries affect the Fantasy projections? Yes. Injured players are removed from the suggestions, doubtful ones are discounted, and the minutes of absent players are shared among their teammates in the Fantasy model.
